Ето ги — по един prompt на екран, в правилния ред за генерация:

1. Onboarding — Welcome screen
Design the welcome screen for Daily Challenges app.
Full screen. App logo/wordmark centered. Tagline below: 
"The only challenge app that actually learns from you."
Two buttons: "Get started" (primary, amber) and "I already have an account" (ghost).
Minimal, energetic. No illustration clutter.

2. Onboarding — Survey (multi-step)
Design the onboarding survey screen, step 2 of 5.
Question: "What do you want to improve?"
Multi-select chips/cards: Physical health, Mental health, 
Productivity, Social life, Finances, Personal growth.
Each option has a small icon. Selected state uses amber.
Progress bar at top showing 2/5.
"Continue" button at bottom, disabled until at least 1 selected.

3. Home — Today's challenges
Design the main home screen for Daily Challenges app.

Top: greeting ("Good morning, Ivan"), date, streak pill with flame icon.

Main challenge card (prominent): category tag, title, 
1-sentence description, duration badge, points badge, 
large "Mark as done" CTA button.

Below: 2 compact bonus challenge cards side by side.

Bottom: tab navigation — Home (active), History, Profile.
Show both light and dark mode.

4. Challenge — Completed state
Design the "challenge completed" moment screen.
Shown immediately after user taps "Mark as done".
Celebratory but not over the top — no confetti explosion.
Shows: points earned (+15 pts), updated streak count, 
brief encouraging message.
Optional: 3 feedback buttons below — "Too easy", 
"Just right", "Too hard".
Amber accent throughout. Auto-dismisses or has "Continue" button.

5. History — Calendar view
Design the challenge history screen.
Monthly calendar grid. Each completed day has an amber dot/fill.
Skipped days are empty. Today is highlighted.
Below calendar: last 7 days list with challenge title, 
category color, completion status.
Streak stats at top: current streak, longest streak, 
30-day completion rate.

6. Profile screen
Design the user profile screen.
Top: avatar initials circle, name, member since date.
Stats row: current streak, total points, completion rate — 3 metric cards.
Weekly bar chart showing last 7 days activity (amber bars).
Settings section below: Goals, Notification time, Language (EN/BG), 
Subscription (Free / Upgrade to Pro).
Clean list style, no heavy cards.


7 Login and Register screen 
Design Login and Register screens for Daily Challenges app.

Auth options: Email + password, Continue with Google, Continue with Apple
(Apple Sign-in is required by App Store guidelines)

Login screen: email field, password field (with show/hide toggle), 
"Forgot password?" link, primary CTA "Log in", 
divider "or", social auth buttons, 
link to Register at bottom.

Register screen: same structure, add name field,
password confirmation field, 
brief privacy note below CTA.

Must-avoid: heavy forms, unnecessary fields
Tone: welcoming, minimal friction






Реда на генерация има значение — започни с Home screen, защото той установява визуалния език. После Completed state (той е вариация на Home). После Profile и History. Onboarding последен, защото е най-различен стилово.
